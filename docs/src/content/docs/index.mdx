JHub Apps (JupyterHub Apps) Launcher turns your JupyterHub into an app
platform: launch anything a user-supplied command can start — a Flask server, a
FastAPI service, or a Panel dashboard — and share it with your team or the
public, with JupyterHub handling authentication and compute.
